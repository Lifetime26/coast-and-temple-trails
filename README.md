# Coast And Temple Trails

Build a modern, mobile-first website for Mangalore Travel Cabs And Taxi Services based on the attached design document and agreed specifications:

Brand & Identity:
- Business Name: Mangalore Travel Cabs And Taxi Services (do not use any reference of Life Time Travels)
- Call Now: 8073565608 (direct tel: link)
- WhatsApp Booking: 6366335594 (direct WhatsApp click-to-chat)

Theme & Aesthetics:
- Color Palette: Coastal Navy & Warm Gold (rich oceanic navy blue header and accents, warm gold/amber badge highlights, clean crisp white cards and background, dark readable typography)
- Modern, clean, and uncluttered mobile-responsive layout

Header & Hero Section:
- Sticky header with brand name, navigation links (Home, Tour Packages, Our Fleet, Why Choose Us, Reviews, Contact Us), Call button (8073565608), and WhatsApp button (6366335594)
- Call-First Hero Banner with headline "Reliable & Affordable Cabs in Mangaluru – 24/7 Taxi & Tour Services", subheadline covering Airport Pickups (IXE), Outstation Trips, Temple Yatras & Local Sightseeing across Coastal Karnataka, and prominent side-by-side Call (8073565608) and WhatsApp (6366335594) CTAs against a scenic coastal/temple travel backdrop

Tour Packages with Categorized Filter Tabs:
- Tabs for: All, Temple Pilgrimage Yatras (Kollur Mookambika, Murudeshwara, Dharmasthala, Kukke Subrahmanya, Udupi Krishna Temple), Coastal & Beach Tours (Panambur Beach, Tannirbhavi, St. Aloysius Chapel, Mangaladevi Temple), and Outstation & Hill Station Circuits (Coorg, Chikmagalur, Bekal Fort)
- Each package card includes title, attractions, duration, and an "Inquire on WhatsApp" button with pre-filled package inquiry message

Fleet Showcase:
- Sedans (Dzire / Etios - 4 Passengers for local rides & airport transfers), SUVs (Ertiga / Innova / Crysta - 6-7 Passengers for family trips), and Tempo Travellers (12 / 17 / 26 Seaters for groups & temple yatras) with specs and WhatsApp inquiry buttons

Service Expertise Highlights:
- 24/7 Airport Transfers (Mangaluru International Airport IXE)
- Experienced local drivers fluent in Tulu, Kannada, English, and Hindi
- Clean, sanitized AC vehicles
- Transparent quotes with zero hidden charges

Pricing:
- Do not display fixed or starting prices anywhere; all prices are decided upon contact via WhatsApp inquiry

Customer Reviews:
- Testimonials highlighting punctuality, clean cars, polite local drivers, and smooth booking

Footer & Quick Booking Form:
- Trip inquiry form (Name, Phone Number, Pickup Location, Destination/Package, Travel Date, Vehicle Type)
- Clicking "Submit" immediately opens WhatsApp to 6366335594 with all entered trip details pre-formatted into the message
- Contact details, coverage areas (Mangaluru, Udupi, Manipal, Kundapura, South Canara), and 24/7 service hours
